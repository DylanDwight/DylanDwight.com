About · Brooklyn, NY · Remote

Design, UX, and front-end development in one pair of hands.

I'm Dylan Dwight. I run Dylan Dwight Creative Technologies, a small practice building clear, responsive digital experiences for independent businesses and creative work.

Open to junior roles Freelance projects Brooklyn, NY · Remote
01

Approach

I combine visual design, UX thinking, and front-end development.

WHAT I DO

Content, interface, and code

On a typical project that means structuring the content, designing the interface, and writing the HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

HOW I WORK

Visitor first, built by hand

I start with what a visitor needs to do, not with a template. I build by hand so I understand every part of what ships, test on real devices, and keep the result maintainable for the person who owns it.

WHAT I CHECK

The details that decide if a site works

Information clarity, mobile usability, accessibility, performance, and real-world business utility. I'd rather tell you a feature isn't worth the budget than build it badly.
